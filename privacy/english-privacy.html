Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2025 • Version 2.0

Avian Driving School, Inc. (“Avian,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal and technical information when you visit our website, schedule lessons, make payments, communicate with us, or use any related online services (collectively, the “Services”). By using our Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

We may collect the following information when you interact with our Services:

  • Contact Information: Name, address, email, phone number.
  • Identification: Driver’s permit/license number, copies of required documents, course certificates.
  • Payment Information: Card or bank details processed securely by our partners (Stripe, PayPal, banks, DaySmart). Avian does not store raw card numbers.
  • Booking & Education Information: Lesson schedules, course selections, attendance, road test appointments, communication history.
  • Emergency or Verification Documents: When submitted voluntarily (e.g., doctor’s notes, accident reports). Retained only as needed for verification.

1.2 Technical & Usage Data

  • IP address, device type, browser, operating system.
  • Pages visited, actions taken, time spent, referral sources.
  • Cookies, analytics data, and tracking pixels (see Section 9).

1.3 Information from Partner Services

We receive limited information from platforms such as DaySmart (scheduling), payment processors, OSHA affiliates, or other verified partner organizations as needed to deliver your chosen services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and manage lessons, road tests, and programs.
  • To securely process payments and manage stored billing methods.
  • To communicate confirmations, reminders, updates, and support messages.
  • To improve website performance, user experience, and internal operations.
  • To comply with DMV and legal requirements.
  • To send promotional offers (opt-out available at any time).
  • To maintain safety and training standards, including use of in-vehicle systems.

3. Communications: SMS, Email & Phone

By providing your phone number or email, you consent to receive:

  • Appointment reminders
  • Road test updates
  • Account or payment confirmations
  • Support responses
  • Optional promotions (you may opt out)

Standard carrier rates may apply. You may opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying “STOP” or contacting us at info@aviandrivingschool.com. Service-related notifications may still be sent when required.

4. How We Share Information

  • Payment & Scheduling Partners: Stripe, PayPal, DaySmart, banks, and similar providers.
  • Affiliated Services: OSHA or partner programs only when necessary to fulfill your request.
  • Legal Requirements: DMV audits, court orders, subpoenas, or compliance obligations.
  • Business Transfers: In mergers or reorganizations, records may be transferred with safeguards.
  • Protection Purposes: Fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and safety of staff and students.

5. In-Vehicle Safety Recordings

Some Avian vehicles may use audio/video recording systems for safety and training. Recordings are used only for:

  • Safety monitoring and instructor training
  • Accident clarification or dispute resolution
  • Compliance with legal inquiries

Avian does not use recordings for advertising or public display. Access is restricted and recordings are kept only as long as reasonably necessary.

6. Use of Artificial Intelligence

Avian may use AI tools to support operational tasks such as scheduling assistance, automated reminders, website chat features, analytics, or fraud detection. These tools assist our team but do not replace human decision-making.

  • Only limited necessary data is processed by trusted AI providers.
  • AI outputs are monitored for accuracy and used responsibly.
  • AI is not used to make legal, disciplinary, or eligibility decisions about students.

7. Website Security Measures

We implement industry-standard practices including:

  • SSL encryption
  • Secure server infrastructure
  • Firewall protection
  • Restricted admin access
  • Routine monitoring and updates

No online system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your information.

8. Account, Booking & Payment Data

When you book through DaySmart or our website, certain information (name, schedule, contact info) is visible to authorized Avian staff to coordinate lessons. Payment information is handled exclusively by secure third-party processors. Avian never sees or stores full card numbers.

9. Cookies, Pixels & Analytics

We may use tools such as:

  • Google Analytics
  • Meta Pixel
  • TikTok Pixel
  • Heatmap or session analytics tools

These tools help us understand website performance and improve user experience. You may control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly without them.

10. Photos, Testimonials & Uploaded Media

If you voluntarily submit photos, videos, or testimonials (e.g., passing your road test), you grant permission for Avian to feature them on our website or social media unless you request removal. Students may request removal at any time.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed to:

  • Provide Services
  • Maintain records for DMV and tax compliance
  • Resolve disputes

Data no longer needed is securely deleted or anonymized.

12. Your Privacy Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your information
  • Request corrections
  • Request deletion (where allowed by law)
  • Request limits on certain types of processing
  • Receive a copy of your data

Email requests to: info@aviandrivingschool.com.

13. International Processing

Your data may be processed in the United States. By using our Services, you consent to such processing.

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect operational or legal changes. The updated version will include a new “Effective Date.”
