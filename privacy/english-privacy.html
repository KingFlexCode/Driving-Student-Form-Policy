Privacy Policy

At Avian Driving School (“we,” “us,” or “our”), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide. This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we collect, use, share, and safeguard your information when you use our website, scheduling systems, mobile applications, vehicles, and related services (collectively, the “Services”). By using our Services, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you register, enroll, schedule lessons, make payments, or interact with our Services, including:

  • Contact Information: Name, mailing address, email address, phone number.
  • Identification: Driver’s permit or license details, photocopies when required, certificates such as the 5-hour pre-licensing course.
  • Payment Information: Credit/debit card details or bank transfer information. Avian Driving School does not store raw payment data directly; payment details may be securely stored by our trusted processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, DaySmart) for billing, recurring payments, fraud prevention, and refunds.
  • Educational: Course preferences, lesson schedules, attendance records, road test appointments, communications with staff.
  • Emergency Documentation: In cases of claimed emergencies, we may collect official records such as doctor’s notes, accident reports, or funeral documents, which may include personal details of third parties. These are retained only as long as needed for verification.

1.2 Usage & Technical Information

  • IP address, browser type, device information, operating system.
  • Pages visited, booking actions taken, time spent, referral source.
  • Cookies and tracking technologies (see Section 8).

1.3 Information from Affiliates & Service Providers

We may receive limited information from affiliated partners and third-party providers (e.g., payment processors, scheduling software, OSHA training affiliates) as necessary to provide or verify services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and manage lessons, packages, scheduling, and road test services.
  • Process secure payments and manage stored billing methods via processors.
  • Verify documentation for cancellations, emergencies, or DMV compliance.
  • Improve services, monitor performance, and analyze usage trends.
  • Send service-related communications, confirmations, and reminders.
  • Provide marketing emails, promotions, and offers (opt-out available).
  • Enhance safety, including review of in-vehicle recordings.
  • Comply with DMV, tax, and other legal requirements.

3. How We Share Your Information

  • Payment & Scheduling Providers: We share information with trusted vendors (Stripe, PayPal, DaySmart, Zelle banks) strictly to process payments and manage bookings.
  • Affiliated Partners: In cases where students use affiliated services (e.g., OSHA courses, partner locations), limited necessary information may be shared.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose data to comply with subpoenas, DMV audits, or court orders.
  • Business Transfers: In mergers, acquisitions, or reorganizations, student records may be transferred.
  • Protection: To enforce policies, prevent fraud, or protect safety and rights of staff, students, and the public.

4. In-Car Recording Devices

For safety, training, and compliance, some or all Avian Driving School vehicles may be equipped with audio and/or video recording devices. By enrolling and participating in lessons, students:

  • Consent to being recorded during instruction.
  • Waive any expectation of privacy inside Avian vehicles.
  • Authorize us to use recordings for safety investigations, training, quality control, or legal matters.
  • Understand recordings are retained securely and disclosed only as required by law or dispute resolution.

5. Use of Artificial Intelligence

Avian Driving School may, now or in the future, use Artificial Intelligence (“AI”) tools and automated technologies to assist in providing services. This may include scheduling support, customer communications, lesson reminders, educational materials, analytics, and fraud prevention. Students acknowledge and agree that:

  • AI use is limited to support functions and does not replace human instruction or decision-making.
  • Certain student information may be processed by AI tools operated by trusted providers.
  • Avian Driving School does not guarantee AI outputs will be error-free, and we are not liable for unintended inaccuracies or omissions.
  • There is no opt-out of AI usage if implemented in our Services.

6. Data Security

We use SSL encryption, firewalls, monitoring, and access controls to protect personal data. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection against unauthorized access or breaches.

7. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide Services, comply with legal obligations, maintain records for DMV or tax purposes, or resolve disputes. Data no longer needed is securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and tracking tools to improve site performance, analyze traffic, and enhance user experience. Students may control cookies through browser settings, though some Services may not function properly without them.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal data.
  • Restrict or object to processing under specific conditions.
  • Request a copy of your data in portable format.

To exercise rights, email us at info@aviandrivingschool.com. We may require identity verification before processing requests.

10. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States, where data protection laws differ from other countries. By using our Services, you consent to such transfers.

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Updates will be posted with a revised effective date.
