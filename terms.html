1. Eligibility

To receive Services, you must:

  • Be at least 16 years old;
  • Hold a valid NYS permit or driver’s license.

2. Safety, Conduct & Student Responsibilities

Students must follow all instructor directions, avoid distractions, behave respectfully, and operate safely at all times.

3. Student Fitness to Drive

  • No impairment from alcohol, cannabis, drugs, or medication.
  • No fatigue, illness, or physical limitation that affects driving.
  • Instructor may cancel a lesson if student is not fit to drive.

4. Payments, Validity, Fees & Refunds

  • Prepayment required for all services.
  • 3.5% processing fee for card/PayPal (Zelle is free).
  • All purchases expire after 6 months.
  • Refunds processed in 7–10 business days.
  • Packages and credits are non-transferable.

5. Cancellations, Rescheduling & Emergencies

  • Lessons must be canceled 24+ hours in advance.
  • Road tests require 72 hours’ notice.
  • Arriving 30+ minutes late = forfeiture.

Emergency proof required within 48 hours.

6. Road Test Vehicle Use & DMV Disclaimer

  • $135 deposit is required.
  • Student must bring all required documents.
  • No meeting at test site; must report to office.
  • DMV decisions are outside Avian’s control.

7. Lesson Duration, Pick-Up Radius & Lateness

  • Standard lesson: 45 minutes.
  • Pick-up free within 5 minutes; outside requires double lesson.
  • Late = lost time or forfeiture.

8. Lesson & Package Expiration Policies

All purchases expire after 6 months.

9. In-Car Recording Devices

Lessons may be recorded. Students consent to recording for safety, training, and compliance.

10. Instructor & Vehicle Assignment

Avian may assign any qualified instructor or vehicle as needed.

11. Weather, Traffic & Operational Delays

Lessons may be shortened, delayed, or rescheduled due to weather or circumstances outside Avian’s control.

12. Progress, Guarantees & Performance Disclaimer

Avian does not guarantee passing the DMV road test or reaching a skill level within a set number of lessons.

13. Student Belongings

Avian is not responsible for lost or damaged personal items.

14. Behavior Toward Staff (Zero Tolerance)

Harassment, threats, abuse, or intimidation of staff may result in immediate termination of services.

15. Liability & Insurance Disclaimer

  • Not liable for student negligence or unsafe conduct.
  • Not liable for incidental or personal losses.

16. Limitation of Liability

Liability is limited to the total amount paid in the last 12 months.

17. Indemnification

18. Arbitration, Choice of Law & Waiver of Lawsuits

19. Non-Transferability
