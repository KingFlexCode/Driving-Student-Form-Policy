Terms of Service & Student Policies

Last updated: November 1, 2025

1. Eligibility

To receive Services, you must:

  • Be at least 16 years old; and
  • Hold a valid New York State learner permit or driver’s license.

By enrolling, scheduling, or participating in any Service, you represent that you meet these requirements and are legally able to enter into this agreement.

2. Safety, Conduct & Student Responsibilities

Driving instruction involves inherent risks. For safety, students must:

  • Follow all instructor directions promptly.
  • Refrain from using mobile devices or distractions.
  • Behave respectfully toward instructors, staff, and the public.
  • Arrive mentally and physically fit to drive.

Unsafe behavior, refusal to follow instructions, or conduct that jeopardizes safety may result in immediate termination of the lesson without refund.

3. Student Fitness to Drive

Students must be physically and mentally capable of safely operating a vehicle. Prohibited conditions include:

  • Fatigue or drowsiness;
  • Use of alcohol, cannabis, or illegal substances;
  • Use of impairing prescription medications;
  • Medical conditions that hinder safe operation.

Instructors may cancel a lesson if a student appears unfit. No refund will be issued.

4. Payments, Validity, Fees & Refunds

  • Prepayment Required: All lessons, road test services, and packages must be paid in full before scheduling.
  • Processing Fees: Card/PayPal payments incur a 3.5% processing fee. Zelle has no fee.
  • Expiration: All purchases expire after six (6) months. Expired services are non-refundable.
  • Refunds: Completed lessons are deducted at the current single-lesson rate. Refunds are processed within 7–10 business days.
  • Non-Transferable: All credits, packages, deposits, and services are non-transferable.

5. Cancellations, Rescheduling & Emergencies

To avoid forfeiture:

  • Lessons must be canceled/rescheduled at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Road tests require 72 hours advance notice.
  • Arriving 30+ minutes late results in forfeiture.

Emergency exceptions require verifiable documentation within 48 hours.

6. Road Test Vehicle Use & DMV Disclaimer

  • A $135 deposit is required to reserve a school vehicle.
  • Students must bring all required documents; missing documents result in forfeiture.
  • Students must report to Avian’s office — we do not meet at the test site.
  • We are not responsible for DMV delays, cancellations, or examiner decisions.

7. Lesson Duration, Pick-Up Radius & Lateness

  • Standard Lesson Duration: 45 minutes.
  • Pick-Up Radius: Free within 5 minutes of the school; outside requires a double lesson.
  • Lateness: Under 30 minutes = lost time. Over 30 minutes = forfeiture.

8. Lesson & Package Expiration Policies

All purchases expire after six (6) months and cannot be extended.

9. In-Car Recording Devices

For safety, training, and quality assurance, some or all vehicles may be equipped with audio and/or video recording systems. By taking lessons, students consent to being recorded during instruction and waive any expectation of privacy inside Avian vehicles.

10. Instructor & Vehicle Assignment

11. Weather, Traffic & Operational Delays

Lessons may be shortened, delayed, or rescheduled due to weather, traffic, vehicle issues, or other operational concerns. No refunds are issued for conditions outside our control, but reasonable efforts will be made to reschedule when appropriate.

Our role is to provide professional instruction and guidance; the outcome depends on student practice, performance, and test conditions.
