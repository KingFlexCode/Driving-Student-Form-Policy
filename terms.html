4. Cancellations, Rescheduling & Emergencies

  • Standard Cancellations: Lessons must be canceled or rescheduled 24+ hours in advance. Otherwise, the full lesson price is charged, or the lesson credit is forfeited.
  • Lateness: Students arriving more than 30 minutes late are considered a no-show, and the lesson is forfeited. If late under 30 minutes, that time is deducted from the lesson length.
  • Pick-Up Radius: Pick-ups outside a 5-minute driving radius from the school require booking a double lesson (90 minutes).
  • Road Tests: Cancellations or rescheduling must be made at least 72 hours (3 days) before the road test. Missed or invalid appointments are forfeited and require a new $135 deposit.

Emergency Cancellations: Students claiming an emergency must provide official documentation with verifiable contact information within 48 hours. Acceptable proof includes:

  • Doctor’s or hospital note on official letterhead, signed and including provider’s full name, license number, phone number, and address.
  • Lab-issued COVID-19 positive test result from a certified facility, with provider/test center contact. Home test kits without official verification are not accepted.
  • Police accident report including officer badge/ID number and precinct contact, or an official insurance claim document.
  • Obituary, funeral program, or death certificate including funeral home name, director’s name, and phone number.
  • Other emergencies: documents from relevant authorities (fire dept, court, employer HR) including name, title, and phone number of the issuing official.

By enrolling, students authorize Avian Driving School to contact any listed provider or authority to confirm authenticity. Fabricated, altered, unverifiable, or insufficient proof will be rejected, and the scheduled lesson or road test will be permanently forfeited. Avian Driving School’s decision on validity is final and binding.
